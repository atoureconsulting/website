About AToure

Built to close
the execution gap.

AToure is a management and consulting company. We build the project and run it on the ground ourselves: a curated creator database, matchmaking and full operational execution under one roof. From talent discovery to campaign delivery, the same team carries every stage, to the same standard.

Our Reach

The Director

Baba Touré

Baba Touré
Director · AToure
The problem was never the idea. It was the in between. So we stepped into the middle and made it personal.

I saw a gap no one was filling. Too many firms treat creators like numbers in a database, focused on KPIs, not relationships.

His work bridges the international and African markets, and not only in social media. It runs through trade, investment and everyday business, opening a route for global brands to build in Africa and for African companies to reach the world.

That network spans West Africa, North Africa, the UK and the Middle East, including deep partnerships with government entities. The goal is global partnership at every level, from ministries and multinationals to grassroots creators and small businesses.

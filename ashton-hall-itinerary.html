How Each Day Runs
  • Tour opens in Ghana (Accra) with the international arrival on 30 Jun.
  • Default wake-up is 08:00. Earlier starts only when a flight or commitment requires it.
  • Each country has one full stream day. Streams run 15:00–20:00 to overlap the US morning with the local afternoon.
  • Travel-out days have no stream — the real flight times do not allow it. Benin is a 1-night stop, so its stream day and travel-out day are the same day.
  • Arrival routine: airport pickup, hotel check-in, SIM cards distributed, full team debrief and security handover with the local ground team in each country.
  • Every flight day shows a flight-detail box with route, airline and times. Confirmed options are named; the rest are TBC.
  • All three scenarios close with the Grand Finale stream in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, with the African Ashton Hall (Ethiopian co-host, TBC).

How Each Day Runs
  • Tour opens in Rwanda (Kigali) on 28 Jun to meet Ashton Small, then flies to Ghana arriving 30 Jun.
  • Default wake-up is 08:00. Earlier starts only when a flight or commitment requires it.
  • Each country has one full stream day. Streams run 15:00–20:00 to overlap the US morning with the local afternoon.
  • Travel-out days have no stream — the real flight times do not allow it. Benin is a 1-night stop, so its stream day and travel-out day are the same day.
  • Arrival routine: airport pickup, hotel check-in, SIM cards distributed, full team debrief and security handover with the local ground team in each country.
  • Every flight day shows a flight-detail box with route, airline and times. Confirmed options are named; the rest are TBC.
  • All three scenarios close with the Grand Finale stream in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, with the African Ashton Hall (Ethiopian co-host, TBC).

  • Réveil par défaut à 08:00. Départ plus tôt seulement si un vol ou un engagement l'exige.

  • Chaque jour de vol affiche une fiche de vol avec l'itinéraire, la compagnie et les horaires. Les options confirmées sont nommées ; le reste est à conf..
  • Les trois scénarios se terminent par la grande finale à Addis Abeba, Éthiopie, avec l'African Ashton Hall (co-hôte éthiopien, à confirmer).

How Each Day Runs
  • Default wake-up is 08:00. Earlier starts only when a flight or commitment requires it (Benin→Morocco pre-dawn flight is the only exception).
  • Each country has one full stream day. Streams run 15:00–20:00 to overlap the US morning with the local afternoon.
  • Travel-out days have no stream — the real flight times do not allow it. Day 1 = arrival, Day 2 = stream, Day 3 = travel to next country.
  • Arrival routine: airport pickup, hotel check-in, SIM cards distributed, full team debrief and security handover with the local ground team in each country.
  • Every flight day shows a flight-detail box with route, airline and times. Confirmed options are named; the rest are TBC.
  • The tour closes with a Grand Finale stream in Cameroon (Douala) alongside the African Ashton Hall co-host (TBC).

How Each Day Runs
  • Default wake-up is 08:00. Earlier starts only when a flight or commitment requires it (Benin→Morocco pre-dawn flight is the only exception).
  • Each country has one full stream day. Streams run 15:00–20:00 to overlap the US morning with the local afternoon.
  • Travel-out days have no stream — the real flight times do not allow it. Day 1 = arrival, Day 2 = stream, Day 3 = travel to next country.
  • Arrival routine: airport pickup, hotel check-in, SIM cards distributed, full team debrief and security handover with the local ground team in each country.
  • Every flight day shows a flight-detail box with route, airline and times. Confirmed options are named; the rest are TBC.
  • The tour closes with a Grand Finale stream in Cameroon (Douala) alongside the African Ashton Hall co-host (TBC).

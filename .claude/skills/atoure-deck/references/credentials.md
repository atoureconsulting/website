## 8. African football context (CAF figures, AFCON 2027)
Use these to show the scale of attention on African football. They are CAF's, not AToure's: label
them as tournament figures and cite "CAF, January 2026".
- AFCON 2025 (Morocco), CAF statement of January 2026: 6.1B digital impressions (headlined as "6
  billion digital views"), 5.2B video views on social media, 285M engagements, 8.5M new followers,
  more than 1M TikTok videos under the official hashtag. Reported as roughly three times AFCON
  2023 (about 2.1B digital views).
- Do not use without a better source: Patrice Motsepe's spoken "2.5 billion viewers", and a 61
  percent global audience growth from preliminary research (the full Nielsen report was pending).
- AFCON 2027, branded AFCON Pamoja 2027: Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda, the first AFCON hosted by
  three nations; 19 June to 17 July 2027 (confirmed by CAF on 2 May 2026; one Ugandan source says
  18 July); 24 teams, 52 matches. Qualifying from September 2026 to March 2027. Côte d'Ivoire are in
  qualifying Group C with Ghana, Gambia and Somalia; match dates differ between sources, check CAF.
- Sources: cafonline.com (AFCON Morocco 2025 digital records), tvcnews.tv, moroccoworldnews.com,
  hespress.com, en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_Africa_Cup_of_Nations, ntvkenya.co.ke, beinsports.com,
  graphic.com.gh. Re-check before a new deck; these move.

# Government Partnerships Case Study Pack

Purpose: reframe delivered projects for a government or tourism board audience,
not a brand audience. Every figure below is already published on the AToure
website. Nothing here is estimated.

## Ashton Hall Africa Tour, 2026

Countries: Ghana, Cameroon, Nigeria, Benin and Rwanda.

AToure delivered partnerships, media, visas, security, transport and full
experience curation in every country on the tour.

Result: 1.57 billion views.

What this proves to a government: a single tour generated sustained global
attention across five countries at once, with AToure managing the state
level logistics, visas, security, transport, end to end.

## IShowSpeed Morocco Tour and AFCON Final Activation

AToure integrated IShowSpeed into the AFCON 2025 Final mascot reveal,
handling institutional facilitation with CAF, state visas, and all on the
ground operations in Morocco.

Result: number one stream of the Africa tour, over 16 million YouTube views.

What this proves to a government: AToure can operate at the federation
level, CAF and FRMF, and inside a marquee sporting moment a country is
hosting, not just around it.

## IShowSpeed Ivory Coast Tour

AToure sourced local sponsors, secured state visas, and produced the
Zaouli cultural activation for global distribution.

Result: 55.8 million Instagram views, 10 million YouTube views, two
sponsors placed locally.

What this proves to a government: a local cultural asset, produced well,
can outperform almost anything else in reach, while also generating local
sponsor revenue.

## Race Weekend, Abidjan

A premium Formula 1 experience across two days at Espace Superkart,
Abidjan, built around a live giant screen projection of the Monaco Grand
Prix. AToure ran this from concept to execution.

What this proves to a government: AToure can design and deliver an anchor
event from nothing, not just attach itself to an existing one.

## How to use this pack

Lead with the country specific project when approaching that country's
ministry or tourism board. Lead with the aggregate, five countries, 1.57
billion views, two federation level partnerships, when approaching a new
market for the first time.

Note: view counts and reach figures should always be re checked against
the live case study pages before sending to a government contact. Do not
publish or send a figure that has not been verified.

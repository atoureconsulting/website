First stop off the boat — Boulay Beach Resort. The Aquaflat challenge takes place at the BBR pool. Floating boards on the water, fitness exercises on an unstable surface, forfeits for every fall. Three-way competition. Content gold.

Aquaflat · Rules
Floating boards in the pool. Squats, lunges, push-ups, planks — unstable surface multiplies difficulty. Forfeits: dive in, singing/dancing penalty, or drink. Audience sets the challenges. Three-way competition.

Segment 06
🌉
Finale · Cinematic · Iconic
Pont Ado
Bridge Run

The stream closes on Abidjan's most iconic crossing. Ashton runs the full length of Pont Ado alongside a Cybertruck or luxury vehicle. Drone overhead. The bridge lit at night.

Why night
Pont Ado is Abidjan's Golden Gate. At 22:30 the city lights are up, the bridge is lit, the lagoon reflects. Far stronger visually than a daytime run.
⚠ Vehicle: Cybertruck or luxury car — source in Abidjan. Bridge access and traffic coordination TBC.
Beat by Beat
  • Activity 1Arrive back in Abidjan from the island. Transfer directly to Pont Ado. No hotel stop.
  • Activity 2Vehicle positioned on the bridge. Drone in the air. Team in position.
  • Activity 3Ashton at the start. Stream intro to the bridge — audience sees it for the first time. Abidjan's Golden Gate, lit up at night.
  • Activity 4Run begins. Ashton runs the full length of Pont Ado alongside the Cybertruck / luxury car. Drone overhead capturing the bridge and lagoon below.
  • Activity 5Run complete. Both Ashtons on the bridge. Abidjan skyline behind them. Stream outro.
  • Activity 6KICK STREAM CLOSES. Final shot: drone pulling back from the bridge over the lagoon. Midnight in Abidjan.

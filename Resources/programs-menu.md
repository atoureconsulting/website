# AToure Programs Menu

Four repeatable offers, each drawn directly from a project already
delivered. The goal is for a ministry or tourism board to be able to say
yes to a defined scope and price, instead of responding to an open ended
pitch.

## 1. Creator and Celebrity Tourism Tour

Model: Ashton Hall Africa Tour.

A multi city itinerary for a global creator or celebrity. AToure manages
visas, security, transport and experience curation, and pairs the trip
with a content and distribution plan built for reach.

Best fit: a country wanting a fast, visible lift in international
awareness ahead of a season or campaign.

## 2. Sports Diplomacy Activation

Model: IShowSpeed Morocco Tour and AFCON Final Activation.

AToure attaches a global creator or talent to a major sporting moment a
country is hosting, and handles institutional facilitation with the
relevant federation plus state visa support.

Best fit: a country hosting or co hosting a continental or global sporting
event.

## 3. Cultural Activation and Sponsor Placement

Model: IShowSpeed Ivory Coast Tour, Zaouli activation.

AToure produces a signature local cultural moment for global distribution
and places local sponsors alongside it, generating both reach and local
revenue.

Best fit: a country with a strong cultural asset that has not yet been
produced for a global audience.

## 4. Premium Experience Build

Model: Race Weekend, Abidjan.

AToure designs and delivers a bespoke live event, built around a global
sporting or entertainment moment, for a host city that wants an anchor
event of its own.

Best fit: a city or country wanting a signature recurring event, not a one
off.

## Using the menu

Each offer should carry a defined scope, timeline and price band before it
reaches a ministry, so the conversation is about which program and when,
not what AToure can even do.

# Government Endorsement Request, Template

Use this for the seven wave one countries: Ghana, Cameroon, Nigeria, Benin,
Rwanda, Morocco and Ivory Coast. Fill in the bracketed sections with the
specific project and outcome for that country before sending.

---

Subject: Requesting your support as we expand the work delivered in
[Country]

Dear [Minister name, or Director General / CEO name and title],

AToure Management and Consulting is the agency behind [name the specific
project, for example: the Ashton Hall Africa Tour, which brought a global
creator through Ghana, Cameroon, Nigeria, Benin and Rwanda and generated
1.57 billion views], delivered together with [government body] in [year].

We are now building formal, ongoing partnerships with a small number of
tourism ministries and boards across the continent, and [Country]'s
support was central to what we were able to deliver.

We would be grateful for a short letter or statement from [government
body] confirming the partnership and its outcome. This helps us show other
countries what is possible when a government partners with AToure
directly, and it helps position [Country] as a leader among the countries
already doing this work well.

We would also welcome the chance to discuss what a renewed or expanded
partnership with [Country] could look like for [next tour / upcoming
event / next season].

With thanks for the partnership so far,

[Name]
AToure Management and Consulting

---

Notes for whoever sends this:

Address it to the operational contact who actually ran the project with
AToure where possible, DMO or federation, not the minister cold. A DMO or
federation contact can often route it to the minister's office for
signature faster than a first approach to the ministry directly.

Do not send a figure in this letter that is not already published and
verified on the AToure site.

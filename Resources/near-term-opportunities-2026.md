# Near Term Opportunities, 2026

## Intra African Trade Fair, IATF 2026

Where: Calabar, Cross River State, Nigeria.

When: 4 to 10 September 2026.

Why it matters now: Nigeria is already a wave one country through the
Ashton Hall Africa Tour. IATF draws trade and government delegations from
across the continent under one roof, which makes it a fast way to open
conversations with ministries beyond Nigeria without a cold email to each
one. Today is 11 August 2026, so there are roughly three to four weeks to
register and plan a presence.

Source: iatf.africa, and Guardian Nigeria reporting on Cross River hosting
IATF 2026.

## Magical Kenya Travel Expo, MKTE 2026

Where: Uhuru Gardens, Nairobi, Kenya.

When: 6 to 8 October 2026.

Why it matters: this is the sixteenth edition and is expected to draw over
ten thousand delegates from around forty countries, one of the largest
tourism trade gatherings on the continent this year. Kenya is a wave two
market with strong tourism budget and momentum, and this expo is a
natural first point of contact with the Kenyan tourism board rather than
a cold approach.

Source: HapaKenya and East African Herald reporting, July 2026.

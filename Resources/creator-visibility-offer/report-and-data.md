# The Visibility Report and the Data Behind It

What the report contains, what each creator and streamer must send, and the
rules for labelling figures.

## Timing

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Before the event | Brief, deliverables, tracked links and the data checklist sent to every creator |
| Within 24 hours | Story insights recorded (Stories expire), live stream analytics recorded |
| 48 hours after | Delivery check: who posted, where, and whether each post followed the brief |
| Day 14 | Full analytics from every creator; full report delivered |
| Day 7, streamers | Replay and clip views recorded |

Write the screenshot and screen recording deadlines into every creator
agreement as a deliverable. Otherwise you will be chasing people.

## Report sections

The sample report uses this order. Each section starts on a new page in the
PDF.

1. **Summary.** Total views, accounts reached (estimated), posts live,
   engagements and rate, link clicks, cost per 1,000 views against budget.
   Posts by format and an engagement breakdown.
2. **Delivery against brief.** Each creator, platforms, followers, posts
   agreed and posted, and a tick or cross for tag, hashtag, key message and
   call to action. Notes on anything missed.
3. **Performance by creator.** Posts, views, reach, likes, comments,
   reposts, shares, saves, clicks, share of total views.
4. **Post log.** Every post with its number, creator, format, time posted,
   clickable link, and its figures. Fields the platform withholds are marked
   n/a, never estimated.
5. **One post in detail.** One post shown exactly as the creator's insights
   show it, across all three tabs. Shows the client the depth held for every
   post.
6. **Audience across all posts.** Countries weighted by each post's views,
   Africa and diaspora split, age and gender.
7. **Where each figure comes from.** A table of source, figures and timing.
8. **Results for the client.** Account growth, hashtag use, tracked clicks,
   promo codes, and the sponsor value panel.
9. **What people said.** A handful of real comments.
10. **Recommendations for the next event.**

## What each creator sends

### Instagram Reels

Screen record all three tabs of Reel insights, at 48 hours and day 14.

| Tab | Fields |
|---|---|
| Overview | Views, accounts reached, average watch time, follows, skip rate, share rate, like rate, save rate, repost rate, comment rate, how long people watched (graph), top sources of views (Profile, Stories, Feed, Reels tab, Explore) |
| Engagement | Profile visits, follows, bio link clicks, likes, comments, reposts, shares, saves, when people liked the Reel (graph) |
| Audience | Age (13 to 17, 18 to 24, 25 to 34, 35 to 44, 45 to 54, 55 to 64, 65 and over), top five countries, gender. Tap each filter while recording |

Instagram limits to know:

- Only the top five countries per post. Smaller markets often do not appear.
  Combine posts by weighting each by its views, and show the rest as "not in
  a top five".
- No city data on these screens.
- Some fields show "--" when Instagram holds data back (often shares, profile
  visits, watch time). Record them as n/a.
- Reach is usually far lower than views. One real Reel showed 410,377 views
  and 46,846 accounts reached.

### Instagram Stories

Record within 24 hours: views, reach, link sticker taps, replies, sticker
taps. Ask the creator to save the Story to a highlight.

### TikTok

Views, likes, comments, shares, saves, average watch time, percentage who
watched the full video, new followers, traffic sources, viewer country, age
and gender.

### YouTube

Views, watch time, average view duration, click through rate, subscribers
gained, country, age and gender.

### Live streams

| Platform | What the streamer can send | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube Live | Peak and average concurrent viewers, unique viewers, watch time, chat rate, new subscribers, replay views, country, age, gender | The fullest analytics |
| Twitch | Peak and average viewers, unique viewers, minutes watched, chats, follows, subs, clips | Little demographic data |
| Kick | Peak and average viewers, followers, chat | Thinnest analytics; screen record everything |
| TikTok Live | Peak viewers, total viewers, new followers, gifts, comments, average watch time | More detail on the replay |
| Instagram Live | Peak concurrent viewers, accounts reached, comments | The replay has normal Reel insights |

Ask streamers for the full analytics page by screen recording within 24
hours and again at day 7, the viewer graph, and links to the stream, the
replay and every clip. The viewer graph lets you mark sponsor and key moments
against audience peaks.

Keep live watching separate from post views in the report. A person who
watched for 40 minutes is worth more to a sponsor than a two second Reel
view.

## What AToure tracks itself

- Post links and times, checked against the brief.
- A unique tracked ticket link per creator (Bitly or similar).
- The client account's followers before and after, and hashtag use.
- Sponsor on screen moments and total screen time, counted from the content.

## Labelling rules

- **Reach** is always lower than views: views count replays, reach counts
  unique accounts. Combined reach across creators can count one person twice,
  so it is estimated.
- **Impressions** for Reels are no longer reported by Instagram. Any
  impressions figure is modelled and labelled estimated.
- **Media value** is modelled from views and typical regional CPM. Label it
  estimated and say how it was worked out.
- **Never estimate a withheld field.** Mark it n/a.
- **Sample reports** carry a red line on page one saying every creator and
  figure is fictional.

## Sample reports already built

Two fictional sample reports exist, same layout, white letterhead with the
AToure and client logos: one for PFL Africa (Kigali) and one for the African
MMA League (Abidjan). They live as private artifacts and PDFs, not in this
repo, because they carry other organisations' branding. To build a new one,
copy the layout, change the letterhead logo, event, creator handles,
countries and comments, and keep every total consistent from post to creator
to summary.

# Pitch Playbook

How to take the Creator Visibility Offer to a new client, and what the first
pitches taught us.

## Before the pitch

1. **Research what the client already does well.** Events, growth, partners,
   broadcast, how they use creators today. Use public sources and keep the
   source list in the speaker notes.
2. **Do not assume gaps.** Ask about them, or frame them as what the next
   stage needs. A pitch that tells a client what they lack invites a defence
   of what they do.
3. **Separate campaigns from visibility.** The client may already run strong
   paid campaigns with good cost per acquisition. That is not what we sell.
   We sell the measured reach of the event through creators and streamers.
4. **Find the commercial angle.** A merger, a new sponsor cycle, an expansion
   into new cities, an investor round. That is the "why now".

## The materials

- **Deck:** eight slides, AToure dark green and gold, Oswald and Public Sans.
  Structure in `adapting-by-event-type.md`.
- **Sample report:** white letterhead PDF with fictional figures, clearly
  labelled. Send it with the deck so the client can see the product.
- **Value proposition:** `README.md` in this folder.

## The email

Order that works:

1. Congratulate them on a recent event, specifically.
2. What is already working, briefly and sincerely.
3. The opportunity, framed as visibility, not as a fault.
4. What we propose, stated as two parts early: managed creators and the
   visibility report.
5. Why the report matters: it turns creator content into numbers sponsors
   and investors act on. Include the revenue model chain.
6. The trial: one event, inside the existing budget.
7. Attachments named and explained: the deck, and the sample report with its
   figures marked as illustrative.
8. Ask for a short call.

Mention the report early. If it first appears halfway down, it reads as an
afterthought.

## The WhatsApp follow up

Short. Says the email has been sent, names the two parts in one line, asks
for a call. Do not repeat the pitch.

## If the client pushes back

- Do not argue their numbers. Thank them, credit the results, and concede
  any point where we were wrong.
- Restate the distinction: their campaign results stand; our offer is about
  visibility across creators and streamers, sitting on top of their work.
- Take every opening they give: guest access for creators, paid streamer
  placements, interest in a sponsor ready report format.
- Keep the report idea alive by offering, not pushing: share how we would
  lay it out, or review their own version.
- Never name a third party as the source of a wrong assumption.

## Lessons from the first pitches

### PFL Africa, October 2026

Pitched a trial at the Kigali Season Finals: managed creators plus a post
event report, with a 2027 retainer to follow.

What happened: the marketing lead declined the trial and the managed
service. They showed their own creator campaign had set deliverables,
messaging and strong measured performance. They agreed that packaging
creator data into a unified, sponsor ready report was a valid point, and
said they would work on it internally. They offered guest VIP and ringside
access for AToure influencers and a separate arrangement for streamers.

What we learned:

- Our premise (no deliverables, no oversight, no messaging) came from
  conversations on the ground, not from their own data, and it was wrong in
  part. Verify before stating it.
- The report is the part they valued. Lead with visibility measurement, and
  offer it on its own.
- Talent access is a real way in, even unpaid at first.

### African MMA League, October 2026

Same deck adapted for AML, an Abidjan based league founded by Ismaël
Bakayoko, who also presides over the Ivorian MMA federation. Built without
attending an event, so slide 2 uses public facts only: ten editions since
the first championship in Abidjan in October 2023, AML 10 in Korhogo in
August 2026, cross border cards with fighters from Cameroon and Morocco.
Sources: Fraternité Matin, allAfrica, Abidjan.net, Sport Ivoire, Koaci,
Sherdog, Tapology. Reports gave three different dates for AML 10, so we only
say August 2026. The broadcaster is not confirmed.

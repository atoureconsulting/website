# Creator Visibility Offer

The repeatable AToure offer for any live event that uses creators: get the
right creators into the event, run them on the ground as the operator, and
turn everything they post into one visibility report the client can put in
front of sponsors, investors and its board.

First built for combat sports leagues (PFL Africa, African MMA League) in
October 2026. Written here so it can be adapted to festivals, concerts,
conferences, tourism campaigns and brand launches.

## The one line

Creators produce visibility. AToure makes that visibility provable, so it
can be sold to sponsors and shown to investors.

Most events can already invite creators. What they cannot do is say what
those creators were worth. The proof is the product.

## The problem it solves

1. **Creator value disappears after the event.** Creators post across
   Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and live streams, and afterwards nobody can say
   what it added up to. At best someone has a screenshot.
2. **Campaign data is not visibility data.** A client may measure ticket
   sales, cost per acquisition and ad click rates well. Those numbers measure
   what the client paid to promote. They do not measure how far the event
   travelled through creators.
3. **Sponsors buy proven visibility.** Without a number, creator content
   cannot be packaged for a sponsor or shown to an investor.
4. **It does not scale with one person.** As a client adds events, cities and
   countries, managing creators informally through one marketing lead breaks
   down.

## What AToure delivers: three layers

### 1. Talent access

- Creators and streamers matched to each event's market, audience and budget.
- Access to the AToure network: global talent (Ashton Hall, the IShowSpeed
  relationships) and regional creators across Africa.
- VIP and backstage invitations organised for talent.
- Streamers placed for co streaming or on site coverage.

### 2. Creator management on the ground

AToure runs this as the operator, not as a middleman.

Before the event:

- Agree set deliverables with each creator: number of posts, platforms,
  formats and timing.
- Brief every creator with the client's key messages, tags, hashtags and
  calls to action (tickets, where to watch, replay).
- Give each creator a unique tracked link, and a promo code where the client
  supplies one.
- Handle accreditation, logistics and scheduling.
- One point of contact for every creator.

During the event:

- Run creators on site and get them into the moments worth filming.
- Make sure sponsor branding appears in shot.
- Check that agreed posts go live.

After the event:

- Check every creator against the brief within 48 hours.
- Collect analytics by screen recording, not screenshots.
- Chase anything missing.

### 3. The visibility report

The core product. Delivered on fixed dates: a check at 48 hours, the full
report at day 14. Full specification in `report-and-data.md`. In short:

- Every post with its link, platform and time, checked against the brief.
- Views, accounts reached, likes, comments, shares, saves, watch time, per
  post, per creator and in total.
- Streamer numbers: peak and average concurrent viewers, watch hours, chat,
  replay and clip views.
- Audience: countries, Africa and diaspora, age and gender.
- Action: tracked link clicks, promo codes, account growth, hashtag use.
- Sponsor value: on screen moments and screen time, plus an estimated media
  value, always labelled estimated.
- Recommendations for the next event.
- The same structure every time, so events can be compared.

Delivered as a PDF for the board and sponsors, with a web version.

## What the client gets out of it

1. **Something to sell.** Creator visibility becomes inventory the
   sponsorship team can package and price.
2. **An investor story.** Audience growth across events, documented.
3. **Lower risk on creator spend.** Pay for agreed deliverables, not
   appearances.
4. **Less load on the marketing team.** One contact instead of chasing a
   dozen creators.
5. **Better decisions.** Which creators, markets and formats actually
   deliver.

## The revenue model

The slide clients need to understand most. Show it as a chain:

**More creators → More visibility → Brand KPIs met → Sponsor and investor money**

AToure supplies the first link and the proof at every link after it. Frame
everything as commercial viability: this is where new money comes from.

## How it is packaged

| Package | What it is | When to lead with it |
|---|---|---|
| Trial | One event, inside the client's existing budget, creators plus a full report | First conversation with any new client |
| Retainer | Creators and reporting across a season or year of events, priced per event or per season | After a successful trial |
| Report only | AToure collects the data and produces the visibility report; the client keeps managing its own creators | When the client already runs creators well |
| Talent access | Guest access or paid placements for AToure creators and streamers | When the client wants talent but not management |

Price the report as its own product. When it is bundled, a client who says
no to management also says no to the report.

## What it is not

- **Not a replacement for the client's own campaigns.** It sits on top of
  paid ads, ticketing and PR. Their campaign numbers stay theirs.
- **Not a fix for something assumed broken.** Research what the client
  already does before pitching. Build on what works.
- **Not invented figures.** Every number comes from creator analytics or
  tracked links. Anything modelled is labelled estimated.

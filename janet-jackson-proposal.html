The Story

A circle that closes.
A chapter that opens.

This is not another celebrity visit. It is the return of a family whose history is already written into Ivorian soil.

1992

Michael comes to Krindjabo

Michael Jackson is received by the Royal House of Sanwi in Krindjabo, integrated into the royal lineage and given the name Amalaman Anoh. In 1995 he receives King Nana Amon Ndoufou IV in Los Angeles. After his passing in 2009, the Kingdom holds a two day royal funeral ceremony in his honour.

2026

Janet returns

More than three decades later, Janet Jackson can walk the same ground. A Culture on Culture documentary episode and a Rolling Stone Africa LEGEND cover, shot in Côte d'Ivoire, turn a family memory into a global cultural moment.

Janet Jackson returned to the African land that embraced her brother, celebrated a new generation of African women, and carried the Jackson legacy forward.

The Timing Opportunity

The Children of Africa Gala.
October 9, 2026.

The Children of Africa Foundation, created by First Lady Dominique Ouattara, holds its 10th Gala Dinner on Friday October 9, 2026 at the Sofitel Abidjan Hotel Ivoire, under the theme Light to Africa: Together for Youth to Shine. It is one of the most prestigious charitable evenings on the continent.

Past editions have gathered more than 900 personalities in a single room: President Alassane Ouattara, former French President Nicolas Sarkozy, Isabelle Adjani, Patrick Bruel, Youssou N'Dour and leaders from cinema, music, sport and business across Africa and beyond. Every edition funds concrete work. The 9th financed a new cardiac surgery wing at the Mother and Child Hospital in Bingerville. The 10th builds the Treichville Youth Center.

Through the Office of the First Lady, AToure can pursue an honorary guest invitation for Janet, subject to official acceptance. Aligning the journey with gala week would seat her at the most exclusive table in Africa, beside the First Lady, in front of every decision maker in the country.

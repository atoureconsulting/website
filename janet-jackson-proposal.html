The Story

A circle that closes.
A chapter that opens.

This is not another celebrity visit. It is the return of a family whose history is already written into Ivorian soil, carried onward to the other West African countries her ancestry reaches.

1992

Michael comes to Krindjabo

Michael Jackson is received by the Royal House of Sanwi in Krindjabo, integrated into the royal lineage and given the name Amalaman Anoh. In 1995 he receives King Nana Amon Ndoufou IV in Los Angeles. After his passing in 2009, the Kingdom holds a two day royal funeral ceremony in his honour.

2026

Janet returns

More than three decades later, Janet Jackson can walk the same ground. Her own lineage reaches beyond it, into Senegal and Nigeria, and the journey has grown to follow that map: Côte d'Ivoire as the anchor, then onward across West Africa. A Culture on Culture documentary episode and a Rolling Stone Africa LEGEND cover, shot in Côte d'Ivoire, turn a family memory into a continental moment.

Janet Jackson returned to the African land that embraced her brother, celebrated a new generation of African women, and carried the Jackson legacy forward.

The Window

The artist's window opens.
15 October 2026.

Ms. Jackson is available from 15 October 2026, and that date now sets the shape of the tour. Nine days of programme run from it: five in Côte d'Ivoire, two in Liberia, two in Nigeria, with Mauritius assessed as a possible closing leg. Earlier October fixtures, including the Children of Africa Gala on 9 October, fall outside the window and are no longer part of the plan.

The relationship with the Office of the First Lady stays live and serves the women and children chapters on their own dates. What the new window buys us is larger than what it costs: a single visit becomes a regional tour, three national press markets instead of one, and a sponsor pool that now includes Nigeria, the largest commercial media market in Africa.

The Ivorian leg rests on institutional access AToure already holds. Liberia and Nigeria are confirmed only as fast as government counterparts in each market are activated, and those channels are being opened now. Scope should be locked early so each leg can be built against a real official calendar rather than an assumed one.

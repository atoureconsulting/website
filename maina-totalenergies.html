The Case

Why Maina for this brief

The TotalEnergies × Le Média Positif Afrique brief calls for a recognised African YouTube creator who can carry the clean cooking story with authenticity, curiosity and reach. Abdullahi Maina does not need to be briefed on food and Africa — he already lives at that intersection.

His @mainavsfood page is built entirely around food exploration — restaurants, street food, cooking — with playlists dedicated to African food. His Nigerian origin puts him inside the story the documentary wants to tell, not outside it as an observer.

"La cuisine comme fil conducteur"

The brief's narrative thread is cooking as a human, sensory connector. Maina has spent years building an audience around exactly that — from street food stalls to home kitchens. This is not a stretch. This is his natural territory.

  • Nigerian origin — Sub-Saharan Africa, directly relevant geography
  • Bilingual French/English — reaches both target audiences
  • Dedicated food TikTok with 1.1M followers & African food playlist
  • MEGA-tier creator with mainstream crossover via @itsyaboymaina
  • 25.8M TikTok likes demonstrates sustained, deep engagement

Proposed Activation

Maina in the field

The documentary follows a 3-act structure. Here is how Maina's profile maps naturally onto each act — no forcing required.

01
Le Constat

The reality of charcoal and wood cooking — indoor pollution, hours lost collecting fuel, impact on women and families in rural Sub-Saharan Africa.

Maina visits families, cooks alongside them on traditional stoves, and lets their reality speak. His Nigerian roots make the conversations natural and unguarded.

02
Les Solutions

Discovery of LPG, TotalEnergies stations, pay-as-you-cook technology, and local entrepreneur networks driving the clean cooking transition.

Maina explores TotalEnergies infrastructure with genuine curiosity — he is a food creator, so the question "how does this change how you cook?" is completely authentic from him.

03
L'Impact

Success stories, entrepreneur testimonials, projection towards the 2030 ambition of 100 million beneficiaries across Africa and India.

Maina closes the story with his food lens — cooking the same dish, before and after. Sensory, emotional, and universal. Exactly what the brief asks for.

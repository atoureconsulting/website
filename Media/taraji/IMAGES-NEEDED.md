## Rights

Publicity stills and red carpet photography are rights managed. Rolling Stone
Africa sits inside Penske Media and has a picture desk that can clear these
properly for a Rolling Stone project. Ask them rather than sourcing images
independently, since the proposal is being prepared for them.

The Weekend

Formula 1, brought to Abidjan.

Race Weekend took over Espace Superkart across two days and turned the Monaco Grand Prix into a destination: live projection on a giant screen, a karting circuit running alongside it, a supercar paddock, a VIP lounge above the track, and 300 guests a day drawn from the city’s young professional and motorsport communities. It was the first premium Formula 1 activation staged in Côte d’Ivoire.

Press & Platforms

Who picked it up.

Race Weekend was covered by Ivorian and panafrican media, by the broadcast partner, and by the country’s events and lifestyle pages.

Scoop AfriquePanafrican digital news. Reported the weekend as a success across TikTok, Instagram and Facebook, with the TikTok cut passing 12,700 likes and framing the venue as Abidjan living to the rhythm of the Monaco Grand Prix.
Canal+ Côte d’IvoireBroadcast partner. Promoted Race Weekend to its own Facebook and Instagram audiences ahead of both days, carrying the venue, the dates and the booking line.
BaabIvorian culture and events guide. Listed Race Weekend at Gasóleo across the two days, crediting AToure Management as organiser.
Superkart CIVenue partner. Ran the event through its own Instagram and Facebook channels to a standing motorsport audience in Abidjan.
Bons Plans Côte d’IvoireIvorian listings and city guide. Carried the announcement to its TikTok following in the run up to the weekend.

NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
AToure Gold              Primary brand gold. Use for the monogram and all gold detailing.
AToure Gold Light        Highlight gold. Use on black garments where the primary reads too dark.
AToure Gold Dark         Shadow gold. Small type and fine rules only.
AToure Gold Pale         Tint. Backgrounds and large soft fills.
AToure Black             Primary black. Warm, not pure black.
AToure Black Soft        Secondary black for panels.
AToure Black Mid         Tertiary warm charcoal.
AToure Cream             Primary light garment colour.
AToure Cream Mid         Secondary cream.
AToure Cream Dark        Cream shadow / edge.
AToure Warm White        Lightest tone. Near-white with warmth.
AToure Text Mid          Body text brown-grey.
AToure Text Light        Muted caption tone.
Race Weekend Red         Race Weekend primary red.
Race Weekend Gold        Race Weekend wordmark gold.
Race Weekend Black       Race Weekend primary black.

CMYK values are a straight conversion and are NOT press-ready.
Ask your printer to match to the HEX/RGB values above, or agree a Pantone
equivalent for the gold before production.
